Data Control

Data Deletion Policy

We respect your right to control your personal and medical data. Learn how to request deletion.

Effective Date: March 26, 2026

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Request removal of your medical scan records (if applicable)
  • Withdraw consent for data usage

What Data Can Be Deleted

Upon request, we can delete:

  • Name, phone number, and email
  • Appointment history
  • Stored scan records (CBCT, OPG, etc.), where legally permitted

Important Note

Certain medical records may be retained for a limited period:

  • To comply with legal or medical regulations
  • For audit and documentation purposes

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of your data, please contact us with the following details:

  • Full Name
  • Registered Phone Number / Email
  • Reason for deletion request

📧 Email your request to: cscanskovai@gmail.com

Processing Time

  • Requests are processed within 3–7 working days
  • You will receive a confirmation once deletion is completed

Data Security

We ensure that:

  • All deletion requests are handled securely
  • Data is permanently removed from active systems
  • Unauthorized access is prevented

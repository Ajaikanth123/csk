Your Privacy Matters

Privacy Policy

Learn how we collect, use, and protect your personal and medical information.

Effective Date: March 26, 2026

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Name, phone number, and email address
  • Appointment details and scan preferences
  • Medical imaging data (CBCT, OPG, etc.)
  • Device/browser data for website improvement

How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Schedule and manage appointments
  • Provide access to dental scan reports
  • Share secure scan links with patients and doctors
  • Improve our services and user experience

Digital Scan Access

Our system provides secure one-click access to CBCT DICOM scans via mobile. All scan data is shared through protected and private links.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security practices to ensure:

  • Secure storage of patient data
  • Encrypted access to scan reports
  • Restricted access to authorized users only

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties, except:

  • With doctors (for diagnosis purposes)
  • When required by law

Cookies

Our website may use cookies to:

  • Enhance user experience
  • Analyze website traffic
